Drawing help

Line, rectangle and circle use two clicks in XY at Z. Finish a polyline with Enter or Finish path. Closed polygon connects its last and first vertices.

Select by clicking an edge, dragging an area or using the Shape list. Drag a vertex at its original Z; click it before inserting or deleting a vertex. Each drag is one undo step. Curves remain analytic.

DXF: points, lines, polylines, arcs, ellipses, blocks, faces and text. Unsupported entities are reported.
